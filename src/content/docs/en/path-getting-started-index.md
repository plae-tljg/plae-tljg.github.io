<p class="intro">Welcome! Here I share my experience as a math graduate.</p>

<h2>Learning Tips</h2>
<div class="tips-grid">
<div class="tip-card card">
<span class="tip-icon">🧠</span>
<h3>Understand, Don’t Memorize</h3>
<p>Mathematics is not a subject of rote learning. Try to understand why each theorem holds and what problem it solves.</p>
</div>
<div class="tip-card card">
<span class="tip-icon">✍️</span>
<h3>Take Notes &amp; Derive</h3>
<p>Write down proof processes yourself, don’t just read. Seeing ≠ Understanding. Being able to write it out is true mastery.</p>
</div>
<div class="tip-card card">
<span class="tip-icon">🔗</span>
<h3>Build Connections</h3>
<p>Try to find connections between different concepts. Mathematics is an organic whole, not isolated pieces of knowledge.</p>
</div>
<div class="tip-card card">
<span class="tip-icon">📝</span>
<h3>Practice Extensively</h3>
<p>Mathematics requires repeated practice. Don’t be afraid of mistakes. Errors are the best teachers.</p>
</div>
</div>
