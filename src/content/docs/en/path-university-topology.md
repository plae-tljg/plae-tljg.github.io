<p class="intro">Topology studies the continuity and neighborhood relations of spaces. It doesn’t care about exact distances or angles, but about the more essential structure of “which points are close together”.</p>

<h2>Core Concept: Properties Invariant Under Continuous Deformation</h2>
<div class="concept-card card">
<p>Homeomorphism - two spaces that can be continuously stretched, bent but not torn or glued together are considered “the same” in topology. A coffee cup and a donut are homeomorphic.</p>
</div>

<h2>Open Set Axioms</h2>
<div class="concepts-grid">
<div class="concept-card card">
<h3>Empty Set and Whole Space</h3>
<p>∅ and X are both open sets</p>
</div>
<div class="concept-card card">
<h3>Arbitrary Union</h3>
<p>Arbitrary union of open sets is open</p>
</div>
<div class="concept-card card">
<h3>Finite Intersection</h3>
<p>Finite intersection of open sets is open</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2>Important Theorems</h2>
<div class="theorem-card card">
<h3>Brouwer Fixed Point Theorem</h3>
<p>Every continuous map from a disk to itself has a fixed point. This is a direct manifestation of the Second Principle - you cannot escape from inside.</p>
</div>
<div class="theorem-card card">
<h3>Jordan Curve Theorem</h3>
<p>Every simple closed curve in the plane divides the plane into interior and exterior regions.</p>
</div>
