<p class="intro">Number theory studies the structure and properties of integers. From the simplest counting to the profound mysteries of prime distribution, it reveals the delicate tension between “finite” and “infinite”.</p>

<h2>First Principle: Integers, Addition, Multiplication</h2>
<div class="concept-card card">
<p>Starting from the simplest object (1) and operation (addition), continuously generating and infinitely expanding. This is the First Principle’s “one-to-many”.</p>
</div>

<h2>Modular Arithmetic</h2>
<div class="concept-card card">
<p>Infinitely many integers, after dividing by a fixed p, can only leave finitely many possible remainders: 0, 1, 2, ..., p-1. This is forcing infinite into finite from outside.</p>
</div>

<h2>Important Theorem</h2>
<div class="theorem-card card">
<h3>Fermat’s Little Theorem</h3>
<p>For any a not divisible by p: a^(p-1) ≡ 1 (mod p)</p>
</div>
