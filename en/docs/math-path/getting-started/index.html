Learning Tips

🧠

Understand, Don’t Memorize

Mathematics is not a subject of rote learning. Try to understand why each theorem holds and what problem it solves.

✍️

Take Notes & Derive

Write down proof processes yourself, don’t just read. Seeing ≠ Understanding. Being able to write it out is true mastery.

🔗

Build Connections

Try to find connections between different concepts. Mathematics is an organic whole, not isolated pieces of knowledge.

📝

Practice Extensively

Mathematics requires repeated practice. Don’t be afraid of mistakes. Errors are the best teachers.

High School Mathematics

Before starting university mathematics, ensure you have mastered these high school basics:

Topic Main Content
Algebra Basics Equations, functions, polynomials
Geometry Plane geometry, intro to analytic geometry
Trigonometry Sine, cosine, tangent identities
Calculus Intro Basic derivatives, simple integrals
