About

I write about mathematics and AI systems. This site is the long-term archive of that writing.

What is here

Series one · Why I Already Knew AI Would Take Over Mathematics A closed arc: starting from how mathematics is learned, which layer of the work machines can take over and which they cannot.

Series two · AI-Maintainable Systems A bilingual set of topics on letting models maintain data and structure while deterministic engines execute. Three words carry the argument: structure, constraints, replay.

This site

  • Fully static, built and deployed to GitHub Pages.
  • Articles are synced from a separate writing workspace: set an article to ready, run the sync command, push.
  • Chinese and English paths, ordered series, KaTeX math, and syntax-highlighted code.
